# Blinkit-Analysis-Dashboard---Report-

## Project Overview 

The Blinkit Data Analysis project was designed to help improve decision-making by analysing Blinkit's operational data, focusing on sales performance, customer behaviour, inventory management, and delivery efficiency. Power BI was used to create a dashboard that integrated data from various sources and visualized insights through interactive charts and metrics.

## Key Objectives: 

•	Understand Sales Data: Identify sales trends, top-performing products, and regional performance. 
•	Improve Customer Experience: Analyse customer demographics, satisfaction, and behaviour to tailor marketing and improve service. 
•	Optimize Inventory: Address overstock and stock out issues by understanding inventory turnover. 
•	Enhance Delivery Efficiency: Evaluate delivery times and success rates to reduce delays. 
•	Enable Dynamic Filtering: Allow users to interact with the data through slicers for indepth analysis. 
 	 
## Situation and Task 

Situation: Blinkit needed a comprehensive data analysis dashboard to analyse sales data and identify areas for improvement in its grocery store operations. 
Task: Developed a Power BI dashboard to visualize Blinkit’s sales data and provide actionable insights for better decision-making. 
 
## Action Steps: 

1.	KPI Identification: Defined key performance indicators (KPIs) like total sales, average sales, number of items sold, and customer ratings. 
2.	Data Integration: Connected Blinkit sales data from an Excel sheet into Power BI for visualization. 
3.	Dashboard Design: Built interactive dashboards with filters for outlet size, item type, and date ranges. 
4.	Visualizations: Used charts and graphs to effectively present data on sales, inventory, and customer behaviour. 
5.	Interactive Features: Implemented features allowing users to filter multiple items and clear all filters simultaneously for a better user experience. 
	 	 
## Result: 

The dashboard provided key insights into Blinkit’s grocery store operations, enabling datadriven decisions for: 
•	Sales performance analysis 
•	Understanding customer behaviour 
•	Inventory management improvements 
•	Enhancing delivery efficiency 
 	 
## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs): 

The dashboard focused on four key performance metrics: 
•	Total Sales: Total revenue generated from all items sold ($1.20M) 
•	Average Sales: Average revenue per sale ($141) 
•	Number of Items Sold: Total number of different items sold (8523) 
•	Average Rating: Customer ratings for items sold (3.9 out of 5) 
 	  
## Dashboard Features: 

•	Filter Panel: Enables filtering data by outlet location, size, and item type for customized analysis. 
•	Outlet Trend Analysis: Displays outlet growth from 2012-2022. 
•	Fat Content Analysis: Breaks down sales by low-fat and regular-fat products, reflecting customer health preferences. 
•	Item Type Distribution: Shows sales spread across product categories. 
•	Outlet Size and Location Analysis: Provides insights on how outlet size and location impact sales performance. 
•	Outlet Type Comparison: Compares different outlets based on sales, number of items, average sales, and ratings. 
 	 
## Key Insights from Visualizations: 

1.	Donut Chart (Product Categories): 
o	Top revenue-generating categories: fresh produce and dairy. 
o	Helped in planning inventory and promotions. 
2.	Matrix Chart (Urban vs. Rural Sales): 
o	Urban areas had higher sales than rural areas, with peak sales during December holidays. 
o	Insight useful for inventory planning and marketing efforts. 
3.	Clustered Bar Chart (Sales and Complaints): 
o	Regions with higher sales also had more customer complaints, pointing to areas for service improvement. 
o	Highlighted underperforming regions needing targeted marketing. 
4.	Stacked Bar Chart (Inventory Turnover): 
o	Premium products had high sales but low inventory turnover, indicating overstocking issues. 
o	Suggested a review of stocking policies. 
5.	Line Chart (Sales Growth): 
o	Sales growth seen over the last four quarters, except during a supply chain disruption. 
o	Led to measures to improve supply chain resilience. 
6.	Slicers (Demographic Filtering): 
o	Allowed detailed customer purchasing analysis, facilitating targeted marketing strategies. 
7.	Cards (Snapshot Metrics): 
o	Showed a 20% increase in new customers and issues with slower inventory turnover, prompting a review of inventory strategies. 
 	 
## Key Business Questions Answered: 

1.	Sales Performance: 
o	Trends: Sales have consistently grown, especially in categories like fresh produce and dairy. 
o	Regional Variations: Urban regions outperform rural ones, suggesting different marketing strategies. 
2.	Customer Insights: 
o	Demographics: Most customers are from urban areas, aged 25-45, helping in targeting marketing efforts. o Customer Satisfaction: Lower satisfaction in high-sales regions indicates the need to improve service quality. 
3.	Inventory Management: 
o	Stock Levels: Overstocking in premium products suggests a need for better inventory control. 
o	Turnover Rates: Faster turnover for high-demand items, guiding stock replenishment strategies. 
 
4.	Delivery Efficiency:
   
o	Delivery Delays: Peaks in delivery delays during high-sales periods suggest better resource allocation is needed. 
o	Success Rates: High success rates overall, but focus needed in areas with repeated delivery failures. 
 	 
## Summary of Insights: 

•	Sales Trends: Sales have shown strong growth, with peak periods during holidays and a preference for fresh produce and dairy products. 
•	Customer Preferences: A significant portion of customers prefer low-fat products, signaling health-conscious buying habits. 
•	Top-Selling Categories: Fruits, vegetables, and snack foods lead sales. 
•	Outlet Profitability: Medium-sized outlets in Tier 3 locations are the most profitable, while supermarkets have higher sales volumes. 
•	Service Quality: Higher sales regions also report more complaints, suggesting the need for service improvements. 
 	 
## Conclusion: 

The Blinkit Data Analysis project using Power BI delivered crucial insights into sales performance, customer behaviour, inventory management, and delivery efficiency. These insights allow for: 
•	Strategic sales planning based on trends and regional performance. 
•	Better customer service by understanding demographic and satisfaction data. 
•	Improved inventory management by optimizing stock levels and turnover rates. 
•	Efficient delivery operations by reducing delays during peak periods. 
This analysis enables Blinkit to make informed decisions to boost profitability and customer satisfaction, while also helping students and analysts understand the importance of datadriven decision-making in real-world business scenarios.
